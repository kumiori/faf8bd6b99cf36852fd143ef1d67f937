Explore a shared world. Reveal a capacity. Express a need. Negotiate an agreement. Accept a responsibility. Act, and make the outcome inspectable.

  1. A potential connection is an inference, not a collaboration.
  2. Every party accepts the same version. Amendments require new acceptance.
  3. Institutional authorisation is separate from agreement between parties.
  4. A commitment is a responsibility. A commit is a recorded state change.
  5. Promises and reported delivery remain separate from verified outcomes.
  6. Shared resources cannot be reserved twice.

This is a local simulation, not legal contracting, production identity verification or real institutional data access.
